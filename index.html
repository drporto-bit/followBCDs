BCD Instagram Directory

A fun way to follow your Board-Certified Dermatologist colleagues on Instagram, boosting our profession’s collective online stature. List your account, follow and recruit others, and climb the leaderboard to earn more followers. Check back frequently to follow new accounts and defend your leaderboard position!

Follow your colleagues

We help you add one colleague at a time (max of 50 per day per Instagram rules).

Tap the purple button to open that dermatologist's profile, follow them on Instagram, then come back here. The next colleague is already waiting, and nobody you've handled comes up twice. Instagram temporarily blocks accounts that follow too many people at once, so stop around 50 in a sitting and pick it up another day. Your place is saved to your account, so you can continue on any device.

Earn your badge

Follow everyone on the list to get a badge and prioritization on the list so you get the most followers.

Once you've followed every colleague, your badge appears here and you move to the front of everyone else's follow queue, most recent badges first, so you pick up follows from colleagues working through the list. When new colleagues are verified, follow them to earn a fresh badge with a new date and move back to the front.

3

Recruit colleagues

Leaderboard

The leaderboard is the order everyone else follows in. Badge holders who've recruited colleagues come first, most recruits at the top. Then other badge holders, most recent badge first. Then colleagues who've recruited but don't have a badge yet, and then everyone else, newest sign-ups first. Follow everyone and share your invite link to move up.
