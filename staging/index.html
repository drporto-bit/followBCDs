BCD Instagram Directory

A directory of board-certified dermatologists on Instagram, open to TBCD members. Add yourself, then follow the colleagues already listed.

1

Add your Instagram

The form asks for three things: your name exactly as it appears on TBCD, your professional Instagram handle, and an email address in case verification needs a follow-up. Your email is never shown on this page. Every submission is checked by hand against the TBCD roster, and this is a volunteer effort run between clinic days, so please be patient waiting to appear on the list.

2

Follow your colleagues

One colleague at a time. Stop around 50 a day.

Tap the purple button to open that dermatologist's profile, follow them on Instagram, then come back here. The next colleague is already waiting, and nobody you've handled comes up twice. Instagram temporarily blocks accounts that follow too many people at once, so stop around 50 in a sitting and pick it up another day. This page remembers your place.

Loading the list…
3

Earn your badge

Follow everyone on the list to get a badge, a check mark by your name, and a spot at the front of the list so you get the most followers.

Once you've been through every colleague, find yourself on the list to create your badge. Your name gets a purple check, and you move to the front of everyone else's follow queue, most recent badges first, so you pick up follows from colleagues working through the list. When new colleagues are verified, follow them to earn a fresh badge with a new date and move back to the front.

Your progress already saves automatically in this browser, so you can close the page and come back. This button copies a link that carries your progress to another device. Paste it into a note or text it to yourself, and opening it on your other phone or computer picks up where you left off.
