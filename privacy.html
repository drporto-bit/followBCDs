Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

The short version: we collect what we need to run the directory: your name, Instagram handle, email, and your activity on the Site. Your name, handle, and standing on the leaderboard are public. Your email and your individual follow activity are not. We may email you, and you can unsubscribe from promotional emails anytime. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how Dennis A. Porto, MD, PLLC ("we," "us") handles information on the BCD Instagram Directory at ig.dermatolo.gy (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to this policy and our Terms of Use.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you sign in with, your sign-in method (Google or email link), and an account identifier.
  • Listing information: the name and Instagram handle you submit, the email on your sign-up, and our verification decision.
  • Activity on the Site: which listed colleagues you mark as followed or already followed, when, your badge and ranking history, who invited you, and whom you invite.
  • Information stored on your device: the Site saves some settings and progress in your browser so it can remember your place.
  • Technical information: our hosting and service providers automatically receive standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and access times.

We do not see your Instagram password, messages, or account data. Following happens on Instagram, not on the Site.

3. What is public

Once you are listed, your name, Instagram handle, leaderboard position, badge status and date, recruit count, and points are visible to anyone who visits the Site, and may be shared by us as described in our Terms of Use. We also publish aggregate figures, such as the total number of follows recorded. Your email address and your individual follow activity are not public.

4. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the Site; verify eligibility; calculate rankings, badges, and recruit credit; prevent misuse; communicate with you about the Site, your listing, and related news, services, and offers; promote the Site; and create aggregated or de-identified information, which we may use and share for any purpose.

5. How we share information

  • Publicly, as described in section 3.
  • With service providers who run the Site for us, such as Google (Firebase and Google Workspace) and GitHub, under their own terms.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of the Site or its assets, in which case information may be transferred to the new owner under this policy.
  • For legal reasons, when we believe it is required by law or needed to protect our rights, users, or others.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Emails

We may email you about the Site, your listing, and related news, services, and offers. You can unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time using the link in those emails or by contacting us. We may still send messages about your account or changes to our terms.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as the Site operates or as needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to remove your listing and delete your account by emailing us. We may keep information where required by law, to resolve disputes, or to enforce our terms, and aggregated or de-identified information and content already shared publicly may remain.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information.

9. Children

The Site is intended for practicing physicians and is not directed to anyone under 18.

10. Your choices

You can edit your submission before verification, stop using the Site at any time, unsubscribe from promotional emails, or ask us to delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, which we will honor as required.

11. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the Site after an update means you accept it.
